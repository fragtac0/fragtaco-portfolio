Aditya Bhoir, also FragTaco.
Creative Editor, Mumbai.

I'm Aditya Bhoir, most of the internet knows me as FragTaco. I'm 26, based in Mumbai, and I've spent the last 6+ years cutting footage into stories.

It started with gaming montages. Syncing frags to beat drops taught me the one lesson that still runs my whole practice: the edit is felt before it is seen. If the cut lands a frame late, everyone notices and nobody can tell you why.

Most recently, I led content strategy end-to-end for Bharat in Germany (editing, packaging, and publishing cadence across YouTube and Instagram) while running a freelance practice on the side: brand films, music videos, and motion graphics packages built from scratch.

When I'm not on the timeline, I'm collecting references: title sequences, anime openings, Renaissance paintings with better composition than most modern posters.
