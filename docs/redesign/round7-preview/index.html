The idea that is not Mars: a line map, like a metro map. Each craft is its own line that starts when I picked it up; stations are the moments; lines share a station where one job covered several crafts. It says "jack of all trades" directly, where the mountain says "one long climb". Hover or tap a station. Rough sketch: the real one would pan, zoom and animate like the map.

Each page gets its own moving ground. All of them move very slowly, stay dark and keep orange as an ember, never a wash. Click a panel to switch between its two options. They are drawn live, so what you see is the real motion.
