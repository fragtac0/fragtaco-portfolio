# Audit tools

Headless Chrome, driven over the DevTools protocol, for screenshots and measurements. Used for the 11 November 2026 audit and for the before and after comparison. The in-app browser pane does not animate while its window is hidden; this does.

```bash
# against the dev server (default) or any URL
BASE=http://localhost:5178 node scripts/audit/cdp.mjs scripts/audit/capture-desktop.json /tmp/out
BASE=https://fragtaco-portfolio.vercel.app node scripts/audit/cdp.mjs scripts/audit/capture-phone.json /tmp/out-phone
# contact sheet from some of the shots
scripts/audit/sheet.sh /tmp/out sheet-loader 4 480 300 load-0500 load-1200 load-2100 load-2800
```

- A steps file is a JSON list. Each step may set the screen (`size`, `dpr`, `mobile`, `touch`), emulate (`reduce`, `net`, `nocache`, `cpu`), navigate (`go`), `wait`, send input (`key`, `click`, `move`, `sweep`, `tap`, `drag`), run code in the page (`eval`, printed with its `label`) and take a screenshot (`shot`).
- Headless screenshots can lag one frame behind. Take a throwaway shot first (the `-warm` names) and use the one after it.
- Each run uses a fresh browser profile in the output folder, so nothing is cached.
- Shots whose names start with `local-` show YouTube thumbnails or book covers. Never commit those; they belong in `docs/redesign/compare/local/`, which git ignores.
- Step files named in the plan that are not here yet (`shelf.json`, `wall.json`, `history.json`, `nav.json`, `fonts.json`, `loader-throttled.json`, `shelf-cold.json`) are written by whoever runs that check, following the same pattern, and committed with the task.

Frame timing inside a step: evaluate a promise that samples `requestAnimationFrame` gaps for a few seconds and resolves with the worst gap and how many were over 20 ms.

- Headless Chrome does not advance timers or CSS transitions while the page is idle between steps (nothing animating). State that depends on a timer (a layer closing, a hint fading) must be read with an `eval` that polls inside the page (a `setInterval` promise), not after a plain `wait`.
- `page-change.json` (written by `make-page-change.mjs`, edit the generator and rerun it) measures a page change: home to work, back, and home to work again at 1440 by 900, dpr 2, CPU 4 times slower. A recorder started in the page before each click reads every animation frame gap from the click until 1 s after the path changes, and long-animation-frame entries say which script caused a long one. Needs the production build on :5180.
- `page-change.json` now reports, per change, the frames while the reveal animates (after the transition's `ready`; the first frame after it is the commit frame, R31, reported apart and not counted): `animMax`, `animOver34`, `animOver20`. Since round 7 (Task 6, R50.1) every change is one radial reveal from the centre of the screen, 3.5 s (`GLASS.ms`), so the file is the glass gate of round 6 (ruling R33) measured at that length; `animMs` reads about 3540. The debug hook (set `sessionStorage["ft:debug-change"]` to `"1"`) gives `__ftChange(to)` (a change to a path), `__ftSettled()` and `__ftFinish()` (what a press during a change calls). The linear kinds and `page-change-kinds.json` / `glass-kinds.json` are gone.
- `transition.json` and `glass-phone.json` (from `make-glass-frames.mjs`) save nine frames of a change each, at 10% to 92% of its length: home to work, work to long form, long form to short form and experience to home at 1440 by 900, and home to work on a phone. The change is frozen at a moment of its length with the debug hook (`__hold`) and photographed, since a running transition is not painted at steady times in the audit browser; `__jump` goes to a page at once with the same finish a press uses. Frames of the work pages show thumbnails (they are named `local-...`: keep them under `docs/redesign/compare/local/`). `glass-finish.json` (same maker) is the way out of a change (spec 4): a key press 1 s in finishes it (focus ends on the dock's current tab when the change began there, else on the page's `<main>`; the key's own action does not happen), a pointer press 1 s in finishes it and a touch tap on the phone profile (390 by 844, dpr 2) does too, and neither activates what is under it (each case counts the clicks that reach the page, `clicks` 0, and has an `ok` verdict: the address, no marks, settled, no errors, where focus is), Back 1 s in (R59) ends the change at once and lands on the page before with no second reveal (a `MutationObserver` on `data-change-kind` sees no radial change, no `glass-p` animation appears, the address and `main` agree, settled within 600 ms, about 320 ms at CPU 4 times slower), a bare modifier and a Cmd or Ctrl shortcut (real key events with `mod`) finish nothing, and reduced motion ends by itself. `glass-coverage.json` (same maker, R62) photographs experience to home (no thumbnails) at 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 0.95 and 1.0 of the change with each page alone first, to show the screen being uncovered; the front's travel is scaled (`TRAVEL`, `UNCOVERED_AT` in `glass-math.ts`) so the farthest corner is at least 98% the new page, with the wander at its worst, only at 0.95 of the time. `glass-interrupt.json` starts a second change, Back, or Back then Forward in the middle of a change and reads that nothing is left over (G, a Back 1 s into a change, also reads that no second reveal runs: no radial change, no `glass-p` animation, settled at once) (`data-change-kind`, `data-glass`, the filter strength, the line's animations, the marks on `<html>`, and that the page shown (`main`) is the one the address names).
- `glass-sim.json` simulates the glass failing in Chromium: the animation cannot be listed, `CSS.registerProperty` missing and a CSS animation that never runs (all end in `data-glass-fallback`, the plain crossfade, and the change ends), an empty computed style (WebKit-like: no fallback, the animation advances) and one picture step failing (the others still work). `glass-interrupt.json` also reads the settled state (`__ftSettled`) and has a scenario in a page whose WebGL context creation is blocked.
- `rings.json` (written by `make-rings.mjs`) checks the work page's rings at 1440 by 900, 1920 by 1080, 820 by 1180, 390 by 844, 375 by 667, 568 by 320, 320 by 568, 1024 by 768 and 2560 by 1440: the arrival (delay, length, start scale of each ring), the smallest gap between any two item boxes over 40 sampled ring phases (each ring and its items' turn-back frozen at a random share of its period; must be 12 or more, 0 pairs under it), the intro inside the stack ring, pictures at three phases for four of the sizes, then hover pausing one ring (and the stack icon not growing), the stack icons (names, no tab stop), frame gaps at rest and with the pointer on a ring (1440 by 900 dpr 2 and 390 by 844 dpr 3, CPU 4 times slower) and reduced motion. The long form pictures are YouTube thumbnails loaded at run time and the short form uses local placeholder art; the file paints neutral generated gradients on the tiles, numbered so uprightness shows. `rings-mode.json` photographs the order mode the running build ships (rebuild with `RING_ORDER_MODE` flipped for the other).
- `ground.json` (written by `make-ground.mjs`, R50.2) checks the Eclipse ground (round 7 spec 5.1) on the production build. The stills in `public/grounds/` (`eclipse-0` to `-3` at 1280 by 800 and `-tall` at 360 by 640, 5 to 30 KB each) are made by `node scripts/audit/make-eclipse-stills.mjs` from the shader itself (it reads `FRAGMENT_SRC` from `app/components/ground/eclipse-shader.ts`, draws it in headless Chrome at clock 0 and encodes WebP; run it again after any change to the shader or to `ECLIPSE` and commit the files). The audit wraps `drawArrays` and counts draws of the ground's own canvas (`__draws`, so the shelf's and the wall's canvases are not counted) and reads: mount time and the lazy `eclipse-engine` chunk; the canvas backing against the CSS size at dpr 1, 2 and 3 (must be equal: one canvas pixel per CSS pixel); draws a second (about 30); frame gaps with the rings turning at dpr 2 and CPU 4 times slower with the loop running and with it stopped (a faked hidden tab); the loop stopping for a hidden tab (0 draws), for a page change (one draw at the commit when the placement changes, none otherwise, then it runs again), for an open layer on the home page and while the home section is off screen (0); the ground leaving with `/experience`; a picture of each placement with the page's own content hidden (`r50-ground-0-work`, `-1-long-form`, `-3-short-form`, `-2-home-section`, and phone ones); grid samples before, during and after a pause, after a lost context (the canvas is removed at once, the still stays) and after a resize (drawn again at once); reduced motion switched on while the ground is live (the canvas fades out over 500 ms, is removed, the still stays); reduced motion, `?nogl=1` and save-data (no chunk requested, no canvas, the still). `init` is added once, in the first step: each extra one would wrap `drawArrays` again and double the counts. Shots named `local-` show thumbnails or covers: keep them under `docs/redesign/compare/local/`.
- `gallery.json` (written by `make-gallery.mjs`) checks the two video walls (R43.5) at 1440 by 900, 1024 by 768 (geometry only) and 390 by 844 (dpr 3, touch), on the production build: every tile's outline inside the screen and clear of the heading, the return mark and the dock (6 px of drift allowed), clicks at 12 points and at every tile centre under reduced motion (the audit warps the layout itself and does the point in polygon test), frame gaps while drifting and while hovering, a real move, click, tap, arrow keys and Enter, the page changes between the walls (the one `radial` change, no canvas while a change runs, the old canvas gone, Esc frees it), and a run with WebGL refused (`sessionStorage.nogl`) where all the links must still show. YouTube thumbnail addresses are replaced by generated numbered gradients (the script patches `Image.src`); the short form's local placeholder art is left as it is; links are not followed, a capture listener records which tile opened.
- `shelf.json` (R44.5) also opens and closes each of three books (the first, the seventh and the last) by click and by keys at 1440 by 900 (and by click and keys at 1024 by 768, 390 by 844 and 844 by 390). Each case starts a frame recorder in the page (`window.__rec`), opens, waits 80 frames, reads the title, the lifted book's box (`data-open-box` on the canvas, in canvas pixels: left, top, right, bottom) and the note's box, closes, and reports the frame gaps for the whole run and inside the 700 ms after the open and the close (`openWindowOver20`, `closeWindowOver20`; frames over 20 ms outside those windows are cover paints that arrive late over the network). The box must lie inside the layer (top 8 px or more) and clear of the note. Arrow presses are sent as synthetic key events from `eval` (the stage listens on `window`); Enter and Escape are real key events. The fit probes read the header's bottom (`data-head-bottom`) and check the tallest spine's top is at least 8 px below it. Each open case also checks the box's top is at least 8 px under the header (`clearOfHeader`, `topToHeader`) and reads the cover texture size (`data-open-cover`) against the drawn size (box width times `data-pixel-ratio`); a flat-fit probe runs at 844 by 390.

- `film` also takes `flat` (a frame whose cells' luma spread is under that counts as bare: a plain ground, whatever its colour), `ats` (saves the frames nearest those times as `<keep>-<ms>.jpg`) and `series` (prints the spread and mean of every frame). With `share` above 1 only `flat` decides.
- `shelf.json` and `wall.json` (R49.2) end with the open and close films of each layer, in four settings (1440 by 900, the same at CPU 6 times slower, 390 by 844 at dpr 2 on touch, and reduced motion). The film's box is the block's own rectangle (`#blkRead`, `#blkWatch`), which stays inside the clip for the whole grow and shrink, and a frame is bare when it is flat (spread under 3). After each open, an `eval` reads `drawnAtMs` (when the layer's `ready` class came) and that the stage was hidden and the picture (`.layer-poster`) fully opaque until then. `shelf-cold.json` does the same for a deep link at CPU 4 times slower and reads the wait, the longest frame gap while waiting, and that the picture goes. The frames show thumbnails and covers: save them under `docs/redesign/compare/local/`.
- R49.2b adds to `shelf.json` and `wall.json`: a film of the open and the close with `?nogl=1` (the poster, then the plain grid at open, then the grid shrinking), and two stalled opens that must show the poster and then the plain grid with no bare frame. The stall is a hook, `sessionStorage["ft:debug-stall"]` (read by the slicer in `app/utils/idle.ts`): `hang` makes the scenes' preparation never finish, a number of milliseconds delays it by that long (counted from when it is first asked for, which is at page load). The layer gives a scene that has not drawn 1.5 s after `open` up to its grid, so `drawnAtMs` reads about 2200; the late variant (12500) then finishes its preparation while the grid is showing and the step reads twice (5 s apart) that the view is still the grid with no canvas. These stall films set the hook in an `init` that stays for the rest of the run, so they are last in each file.
- `dock-row.json` (R49.3) ends with the dock's highlight and the open tab (written by `make-dock-pill.mjs`, which appends and replaces its own steps, marked `"group": "dock-pill"`): on every dock change the highlight's place against the dock's tab row is read every frame (its left moves one way only with no overshoot, its top stays level, it ends under the current tab; reduced motion puts it on the new tab at once), and a click, Enter and Space on the open tab (the dock's Work on `/work`, the row's Long form, Short form and My experience on their own addresses) leave the address, history, `<main>` and focus alone with no page change. `nav.json` also presses the open tab on the rings, the row and the two walls and reads that nothing changes.
- `home-edge.json` and `home-tabs.json` (R50.3, hand-written) check the home hero's polish on the production build. `home-edge.json` scrolls so the painting's lower edge is mid-screen at 1440 by 900 (dpr 2) and 390 by 844 (dpr 3, touch) in three settings (live painting, reduced motion = the still, `?nogl=1` = the plain image) and saves `r7-edge-<size>-<setting>` (the page below the seam shows the books and videos blocks, whose pictures are covers and thumbnails: crop to the top 80% before tracking them). The seam is read from the pictures by hand: the mean luma of the rows 2 to 8 px above and below the seam differ by under 1 level (11.0 against 10.6 to 11.0). `home-tabs.json` sweeps the pointer onto each tab (`card left hot` / `card right hot` are printed), then freezes the card's slide at 40 to 45% with the Web Animations API (`getAnimations()` filtered to the `transform` transition, paused, `currentTime` set) and photographs it, opening About and Credits and closing About; the printed durations must read `700 cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)`.
